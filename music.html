OK here are my full thoughts of the Noah Kahan Concert in SF.
First the Set List:

American Cars - ok for this song I didnt know it fully, just the chorus part but alas it has been stuck in my head for the last few days. 7.5/10

Doors - again didnt really know this song but it didnt give me a full impression, and tbh i couldnt really hear it all too well. Will be listening to it again soon though. 5/10

All My Love - If you guys havent caught on yet, im not really versed in the Noah Kahan cultere. But I went bc if i like one of his songs i REALLY like it. This song Ive been hearing about the "WHERE" ever since it came out. But in all honestly this is when I started getting emotional. 7.5/10

Deny Deny Deny - Dont have much to say about this song, didnt know it much but again has been stuck in my head. 9/10

Staying Still - When he played this song I had never heard it before, it didnt really affect me much but last night i was tempted to listen to it again and wow. Noah Kahan can really verbalize the feeling it is to, at least to me, that feeling when all your friends move away and youre slowly saying bye to each and everyone of them till your the last one. 7/10

Haircut - This song is one of my favorites of his, and I was so excited to hear it live. It was amazing. Nuff said 10/10

Downfall & Dashboard - idk these song, and they didnt really stick on me. 5/10

DIAL DRUNK - Ugh this mightve been my favorite, the song was amazing live and at the beginning he gets "arrested" and he sings a good portion of the song in the back of a 'cop car'. As a theater kid LOVED IT. 10/10

Willing and Able - nothing to say other than i was about to jump off the roof. Oh also he performed this on top of the roof that was on stage. 10/10

Porch Light - This song will always be about the last episode of mondern family. 9/10

Orbiter - In my video of this song you can hear me scream "ROCKYYYY!!! GRACE!!!!" and then was followed by me crying. 10/10

Call your Mom - So apparently this was the surprise song. Yall. Can we agree, him going from Willing and Able, to Porch Light, to orbiter, to Call Your Mom was just...mean. Noah come on man. 11/10

Paid Time Off - Yeah idk this song srry. 6/10

Dan - As someone who was already sobbing at this point, your best friend grabbing you to this song and then hearing the lyrics "And we're so alone most of the time, most of the time we dont have anyone. Where do we go when we die? I wouldnt mind right here." genuinely was about to be my 13th reason why. 11/10

The View Between Villages, Northan Attittude, The Great Divide, New Persepctive - I call this group of songs, 'Songs about having trauma tied to your high school life but also missing it because of nostalgia. also still crying now, and New Perspective has always been a song i wanted to film a video to. 11/10

End Of August - Loved this song, we need more just piano songs from artist. 11/10 'Anything you need, I will provide.'

Stick Season - No Notes 11/10

The only thing i will say is that Noah ended up cutting like 2 or 3 songs, one of them being She Calls Me Back, and I am so disappointed i couldnt do 6-7 with him. but other than that i understand why he cut his show short. Bros been sick for like a week, hope he gets better soon. If you plan on going to this concert, a) emotional therapy before and after b) drink lots of water c) bring tissues.
